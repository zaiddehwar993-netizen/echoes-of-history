Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a website take to build?

Most projects take 5–14 days depending on scope — a simple landing page is faster, while a full platform with a database (like a job board or dashboard) takes longer.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing depends on features, design complexity, and whether you need ongoing support. Message me your requirements on WhatsApp and I'll send a clear quote.

What technologies do you use?

Modern, fast, and SEO-friendly stacks — HTML/CSS/JS for lighter sites, and Next.js with Supabase for full web apps with logins, dashboards, or databases.

Do I get support after launch?

Yes — every project includes post-launch support for bug fixes, and ongoing maintenance plans are available if you need regular updates.

How does payment work?

Typically a portion upfront to begin work, with the remainder on delivery. Exact terms are agreed on before the project starts.
